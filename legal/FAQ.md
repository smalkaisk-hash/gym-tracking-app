# FitPulse FAQ

FitPulse is a personal gym tracker: you log your workouts and body weight, and it
shows your training history, muscle balance and progress over time.

**Can't find your answer here?** Email **smalkaisk@gmail.com** and we'll help.

This page explains how the app works. It is not part of the
[Terms of Service](/terms.html) or the [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) — where a
question touches your data or your rights, those documents are the ones that bind.

## Using the app

### How do I start a workout?

The first time, the home screen asks you to pick a training split (Push / Pull /
Legs, Upper / Lower, Full Body, and others). After that:

1. On the home screen, tap the day you're training.
2. Enter your body weight if you're asked for it.
3. The session opens pre-filled with that day's usual exercises. Type your reps
   and weight for each set, and add or remove exercises as you go.
4. Tap **Finish** when you're done. Exercises you didn't touch are dropped
   automatically.

You can't accidentally navigate away from a workout in progress — the bottom tab
bar is hidden until you finish or discard it.

### I closed the app mid-workout. Did I lose it?

No. An unfinished session is saved on the device and restored when you reopen the
app. If it has been sitting for a long time the sets are still kept — only the
session timer is switched off, so the workout won't be logged with a misleading
duration.

### Can I log an exercise with no weight?

Yes, for core exercises. Leave the weight field empty and log the reps on their
own. An empty weight means bodyweight, which is different from a weight of zero, so
don't type `0` for a bodyweight set.

### Weights are showing in the wrong unit

Open **Settings → Units** and switch between kilograms and pounds. This changes
the display only. Every weight is stored in kilograms, so switching units never
rewrites your logged workouts.

### How do I rename my training days?

On the home screen, choose **Rename days** and type your own label for each day.
This is display-only: your history, stats and filters keep working, because the
underlying day type never changes.

### A widget disappeared from my dashboard

You can hide widgets per screen in **Settings → Widgets**, and the same widget can
be hidden on the dashboard while staying visible on Progress. Turn it back on from
the same list. Starting a workout and the Progress filter can't be hidden.

### What does the muscle chart count?

The donut on the dashboard and Progress screen totals your training by muscle
group. **Settings → Display** lets you choose whether it counts sets, total volume
(reps times weight), or sessions. Counting sessions deliberately adds up to more
than your session count, because one workout is counted once for every muscle
group it worked.

### Does FitPulse work offline?

Yes. Changes you make offline are saved on the device and upload the next time you
have a connection. While something is waiting to sync you'll see a banner with a
count, and a **Not synced** chip on the affected workout in History. There is no
automatic retry yet — a change that failed to upload goes up on your next
successful save or when you tap **Retry**.

## Your account and your data

### How do I change my email address or password?

Both are in **Settings → Account**.

- **Email:** you'll be asked to confirm the change from *both* the old and the new
  address, so a typo can't lock you out.
- **Password:** you have to enter your current password as well as the new one.

### What syncs between my devices?

Your workouts, body-weight log and settings are stored on the server and appear on
every device you sign in on. The offline cache in each browser is just a local
copy of that. Your light/dark theme choice is per-device.

### How do I get a copy of my data?

**Settings → Data → Export backup** downloads a JSON file containing your workouts,
your body-weight log, your settings and your account details, including the record
of the consent you gave at sign-up. This is also our answer to a data-access or
data-portability request under the GDPR.

### How do I import a backup?

**Settings → Data → Import backup.** Importing *replaces everything* on your
account with the contents of the file, so only import a backup you exported
yourself. A file that would replace your data with nothing is rejected.

### What's the difference between signing out and deleting my data?

- **Sign out** (Settings → Account) clears the copy of your data on the device
  you're using. Your data stays on the server and on your other devices.
- **Delete all my data** (Settings → Data) permanently deletes every workout,
  weigh-in and setting from the server. It cannot be undone.

### How do I delete my whole account?

See [Delete your account](/delete-account.html). You can do it from inside the app
or, if you can't sign in, by email. Deletion is permanent and there is no backup
to restore from, so export first if you want to keep your history.

### I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, choose **Forgot password?** and enter your email. You'll be
sent a link to set a new one.

## Privacy and legal

### What data does FitPulse collect, and do you sell it?

Your email address, the workouts and body weight you log, and your settings. We do
not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. The
[Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) is the full account of what we collect, why, and
who it is shared with.

### Is my workout data treated as health data?

Yes. Because it can reveal information about your body and your health, we treat it
as health data and ask for your explicit consent to process it — as a separate
tick-box from accepting the Terms — when you create your account. The
[Consumer Health Data Privacy notice](/consumer-health-data-privacy.html) sets out
the extra rights that come with that.

### Does the app send crash reports or analytics?

No. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) says so, and that statement is kept true by
a build check — the app cannot ship crash reporting without the policy being
updated in the same change.

### Why am I being asked to accept updated Terms?

When we change the [Terms of Service](/terms.html) or the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) in a way that matters, we email you at least 30
days beforehand and then ask you to accept the new version. You can keep using the
current version until the change takes effect, and you can always export your data
or delete your account instead of accepting.
