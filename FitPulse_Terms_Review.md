# FitPulse Terms of Service Review

## 1. Define "commercial use"

Section 6 only says **personal, non-commercial fitness tracking**.
Define what counts as commercial use (e.g. trainers, gyms, researchers,
influencers).

## 2. Account sharing

State that violating the one-account rule may result in suspension or
termination.

## 3. Export format permanence

Avoid promising only JSON. Use wording like "JSON or another documented
machine-readable format."

## 4. Import compatibility

Clarify whether backups are guaranteed to import across future versions.

## 5. Immediate deletion

Replace "immediate deletion" with "initiated immediately and completed
as soon as reasonably practicable."

## 6. Offline sync conflicts

Explain how conflicts are resolved when multiple offline devices edit
the same data.

## 7. Email verification

Require email verification to reduce impersonation risks.

## 8. Password recovery

Clarify how password recovery works and that access depends on the
registered email.

## 9. Data retention after deletion

Explain treatment of logs, backups, caches, and monitoring systems.

## 10. Inactive accounts

State whether inactive accounts may be deleted after prolonged
inactivity.

## 11. Automated abuse detection

Clarify whether automated systems may temporarily suspend accounts.

## 12. Future APIs

Reserve the right to apply separate API terms and rate limits.

## 13. Service discontinuation

Address situations where 30 days' notice is impossible (bankruptcy,
provider failure).

## 14. Force majeure vs export

Clarify how export obligations interact with force majeure events.

## 15. Sole operator

Avoid wording that becomes inaccurate if additional staff are hired.

## 16. Browser support

Reference a supported browser/device list instead of implying universal
compatibility.

## 17. Device compatibility

Clarify supported operating systems and versions.

## 18. Time zones

Describe how timestamps, weekly goals, and streaks behave across time
zones.

## 19. Device clock manipulation

Specify whether server time or device time is authoritative.

## 20. Fraudulent data

Clarify that calculations depend on user-entered data.

## 21. Custom exercises

Clarify ownership of user-created exercise names and content.

## 22. Usernames

Add restrictions on misleading or trademark-infringing usernames.

## 23. Reverse engineering

Consider whether benchmarking or public performance comparisons should
be addressed.

## 24. Feedback licence

Specify rights to use voluntary user feedback.

## 25. Multiple accounts

Clarify exceptions for forgotten or testing accounts.

## 26. Invalid email addresses

Clarify responsibility when notices cannot be delivered due to outdated
email addresses.

## 27. Notification failures

Address spam filters and full mailboxes in addition to delivery
failures.

## 28. Rate limits

Define what constitutes excessive usage where practical.

## 29. Material feature

Define what "material feature" means.

## 30. Maintenance notice

Clarify what "reasonable advance notice" means where possible.

## 31. Security promises

Avoid implementation-specific promises that may become outdated.

## 32. Aggregated analytics

Define what qualifies as aggregated or anonymous data.

## 33. Export completeness

Future-proof export obligations for newly added data types.

## 34. Consumer vs business users

Clarify treatment of business users.

## 35. Sanctions

Specify which sanctions or restricted-party lists apply.

## 36. Third-party providers

Reserve the right to change infrastructure providers.

## 37. Accuracy disclaimers

Differentiate user-input errors from software defects.

## 38. Free app wording

Avoid wording that may conflict with future paid plans.

## 39. Export abuse

Reserve the right to limit excessive export frequency.

## 40. Future AI features

Extend health disclaimers to future AI-generated recommendations.

## Highest-priority improvements

1.  Remove implementation-specific technical promises.
2.  Future-proof export/import commitments.
3.  Clarify deletion timing and retention.
4.  Define ambiguous concepts.
5.  Add provisions for future growth (AI, APIs, premium features,
    infrastructure).
6.  Clarify operational edge cases.
